# MeowDB price suggestions

These are a small checked reference set, not a full database import or a live price feed. Public MeowDB pages were read on 2026-10-16. That date is our website check, not an in-game verification date. Suggestions stay local, remain editable, and require **Save item / price**. Existing outings keep their frozen prices.

[Arturo's recorded shop](https://meowdb.com/msclassic/npcs/509) independently lists the potion purchase prices and Pet Food at 35. Pet Food prices vary: its page also lists Doofus at 30 and Valen at 40. Use the price you actually pay when it differs. The quoted potion prices also appear in MeowDB's [NPC shop database](https://meowdb.com/msclassic/npc-shops).

The Subi page distinguishes a recharge rate of 0.3 from a purchase price of 500 and a sell-back listing of 250. Only the explicit recharge rate is used for existing ammunition. Refilling 500 missing stars at the listed rate costs 150 mesos. Buying or acquiring additional reusable star stacks introduces value beyond this ammunition model and needs separate accounting; the suggestion is intended for the user's existing stacks.

The numeric URL suffixes are MeowDB page identifiers, not verified internal game IDs. The Subi page explicitly records the earlier hyphenated name, so both names are supported. Other matches require the exact item name and kind, ignoring case and outside whitespace. Generic `HP Potion` and `MP Potion` are not assigned prices. Rename them to the actual potion or add a specific catalog item before applying a suggestion.

Player-market estimates are not used. These values concern NPC sales or replacement costs. Manual overrides remain available, and no values are fetched automatically during startup or use.
